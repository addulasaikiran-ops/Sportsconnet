## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-contrast sports-tech networking environment engineered for athletes, coaches, scouts, and athletic organizations. It bridges the structural authority of institutional sports bodies with the kinetic drive of high-performance physical training.

### Emotional Tone & Target Experience
- **Championship Rigor:** Utilitarian, reliable, and professional. Avoids amateur social gimmicks, heavy visual noise, or saturated carnival gradients.
- **Electric Performance:** Strategically deployed pitch green highlights against calm slate white canvas surfaces evoke freshly manicured turf, crisp stadium lighting, and athletic precision.
- **Decisive Velocity:** Fast visual hierarchy that allows scouts and recruiters to parse metrics, sport roles, highlight reels, and application deadlines within seconds.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Modern Sports-Tech Precision**—a functional synthesis of corporate software clarity and athletic gear minimalism. The interface relies on pure white modular containers bounded by hairline borders, cool slate neutral grounds, deep midnight navy structural anchors, and surgical applications of emerald green to signal active progress and verified milestones.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable sports identity while maintaining strict accessibility and utility:

- **Primary Emerald Green (`#16A34A`):** The kinetic pulse of the UI. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons, verified badge indicators, progression markers, and active states. It communicates peak performance, open paths, and confirmed achievements.
- **Deep Navy Structural Base (`#101828`):** The anchoring foundation. Used for dark hero zones, top bar headers, and critical high-contrast text. It provides institutional authority, preventing the green from appearing juvenile or overly casual.
- **Tertiary Kinetic Lime (`#C8F169`):** High-energy brand punctuation mark used sparingly for spark icons, streak badges, and hero graphic overlays. Never used for body text or unbordered buttons.
- **Canvas Neutral (`#F8FAFC`):** Cool slate background that eliminates bright-white screen fatigue while maintaining crisp separation from container surfaces.

### Functional Roles & Rules
- **Text on White:** Continuous body text must use `#172033` (AAA contrast) or `#33445B`. Secondary descriptions use `#667085`.
- **Green Text Accessibility:** Emerald text on light canvas surfaces must never use the bright `#22C55E` accent; it must strictly utilize `#15803D` (5.3:1 contrast against `#FFFFFF`).
- **Surface Layering:** Neutral canvas `#F8FAFC` holds pure `#FFFFFF` cards, with `#F1F5F9` providing contrast for table headers, inactive tags, and card footers.

## Typography

The type system is built on **Inter**, delivering geometric discipline, neutral precision, and exceptional legibility across dense data matrices and athletic stats.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tight Display Tracking:** Headings from 20px upward enforce tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to project condensed athletic impact and unity.
- **Disciplined Reading Rhythm:** Body text is calibrated to 15px with a generous 24px line height (1.6 ratio), allowing multi-paragraph sports updates, bio narratives, and scout evaluation notes to remain legible under rapid scrolling.
- **Technical Eyebrows:** Micro-labels, category indicators, and section kickers (`label-caps`) use 11px uppercase bold styling with expanded tracking (`+0.08em`) to mimic sports timing displays and jersey lettering without losing legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured fluid-responsive grid system designed around a 4px modular cadence.

### Grid & Max Width Rules
- **Standard Content Canvas:** Max width `1240px` centered with dynamic page margins.
- **Narrow Form/Auth Views:** Max width `440px` (or `880px` for multi-step onboarding).
- **Desktop Feed Architecture:** Asymmetric 2-column layout pairing a flexible main feed (`minmax(0, 1fr)`) with a sticky `300px` right-rail panel for active events, trending athletes, and application deadlines.
- **Athlete Discovery Grid:** 3-column auto-fill grid on desktop (`repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))`), dropping to 2 columns on tablet (`< 1000px`), and 1 column on mobile (`< 640px`).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Strategy
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout. Desktop sidebar is unmounted in favor of a fixed 64px bottom tab navigation. Page margins drop to 16px. Touch targets enforce a minimum of 44x44px.
- **Tablet (641px – 1000px):** Sidebar collapses to a 72px icon-only rail. Modals and feed panels occupy full width with 24px margins.
- **Desktop (1001px+):** Left vertical navigation expands to 246px fixed width. Sticky top search bar (`68px` height) remains anchored.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through **low-contrast hairline outlines and subtle ambient drop shadows**, avoiding aggressive skeuomorphic drop shadows, saturated blurs, or heavy floating elements.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Neutral slate canvas (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow, no outline.
- **Level 1 (Structural Card):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by a 1px hairline border (`#E4E7EC`) and an ambient tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Floating Menus):** Applied to active talent cards on hover, open filter dropdowns, and message flyouts:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.05);`
  The hairline border simultaneously transitions to `#CFD7E2`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Deep navy translucent backplate (`rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.60)`) paired with a white modal container utilizing `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.24)`.
- **Glassmorphic App Bar:** Sticky desktop header and mobile bottom bar use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border of `1px solid #E4E7EC`.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a disciplined rounded shape profile (`Level 2`), conveying modern technical refinement rather than toy-like bubbles or severe brutalist corners.

### Component Radii Mapping
- **Micro Radii (4px – 6px):** Keyboard shortcut chips (`<kbd>`), inner checkmarks, message bubble connecting spurs, and status pills.
- **Default Control Radii (8px – 10px):** Primary/secondary buttons, input text fields, select dropdowns, search bars, and post composer containers.
- **Card Containers (12px – 16px):** Feed cards, opportunity items, athlete profile headers, and modal panels.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Profile avatars, online presence indicators, sports category filter chips, and verified checkmarks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Emerald Action:**
  - Background `#16A34A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `10px`, font weight `600`.
  - Hover: Background darkens to `#15803D`.
  - Active: Background compresses to `#166534` with `transform: scale(0.98)`.
  - Focus Ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Structural Navy:**
  - Background `#101828`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `10px`. Hover: `#1E293B`.
- **Outline Button:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E7EC`, text `#172033`. Hover: Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CFD7E2`.
- **Sizes:**
  - Small: Height 34px, padding 0 12px, font-size 13px.
  - Default: Height 40px, padding 0 16px, font-size 14px.
  - Large: Height 48px, padding 0 22px, font-size 15px.

### Input Fields & Search Controls
- Height 42px (44px for auth), border `1px solid #CFD7E2`, radius `10px`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#172033`, placeholder `#8592A3`.
- Focus state: Border `#16A34A`, ambient glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.20)`.
- Global search includes right-aligned inline `<kbd>⌘ K</kbd>` pill styled with `#F1F5F9` background and `#667085` text.

### Filter Chips & Badges
- Filter chips use 32px height, pill radius (`9999px`), border `1px solid #E4E7EC`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#667085`.
- Selected state adopts `#ECFBF2` mint surface, `#16A34A` border, and bold `#15803D` green text.
- Metric and streak badges use `#FFF5E6` container with `#D97706` text and flame graphic.

### Cards
- **Feed Post Card:** 20px padding, white surface, 1px `#E4E7EC` border, 12px radius. Features 40px circular avatar, name in `#172033` (semibold), secondary sport role in `#667085`, embedded media preview with 8px radius, and a 3-column interaction footer (Appreciate / Comment / Save) divided by a top hairline border.
- **Athlete Talent Card:** Features a 100px sport discipline gradient header, an overlapping 64px avatar with a 3px white halo, bold athlete name, discipline/position metadata, location pin, and a full-width secondary outline button "View profile".
- **Opportunity Card:** 3-column responsive layout: 52px square sport icon block, center details column with category tag in uppercase bold emerald green, and right column with closing date and "View & apply" button.

### Athlete Avatars
- Sizing scale: 28px (`xs`), 36px (`sm`), 44px (`md`), 64px (`lg`), 112px (`xl` for profile header).
- Initial avatars utilize sport-tinted background tokens: `.avatar-blue` (`#DCEEFF`), `.avatar-green` (`#E0F3D7`), `.avatar-navy` (`#DCE7F2`).
- Verified badges are 16px circular icons with `#16A34A` fill anchored to the bottom-right quadrant of the avatar.

### Messaging Bubbles
- **Incoming Message:** `#F1F5F9` surface, `#172033` text, 12px border radius with top-left corner reduced to 4px.
- **Outgoing Message:** `#101828` navy surface with white text (or `#ECFBF2` mint surface with `#15803D` text), 12px radius with top-right corner reduced to 4px.
